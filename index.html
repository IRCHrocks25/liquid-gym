The Defining Catalyst

She Was the Patient Before She Was the Founder.

In 2008, Karen Snyder broke her leg playing hockey.

Months of land based therapy progressed slowly. Then everything changed.

She got in the water. Movement came back. Confidence came back.

What if water is not the backup plan, but the answer?

Karen was not only a patient. She was already a builder.

Years working as a builder of businesses and training programs, including the Canadian Red Cross's own Adapted Aquatics program in New Brunswick.

Karen was not alone in knowing water.

Irene Hammerich had already spent decades building her own aquatic programs.

At St. Francis Xavier University, then the North York YMCA.

Together, they brought more than forty years of combined aquatic experience to LiquidGym, which they co-founded in November 2013.

Twelve years and ten thousand patients later, that question became the method behind everything ATI does today.

The Aquatic Philosophy

Function First. Environment Second.

Water is the great equalizer.

Standing chest deep, the body carries only twenty percent of its weight.

The water removes what land cannot.

Then it returns resistance from every direction, building strength without the impact.

This is the principle behind Functional Movement Aquatic Therapy (FMAT™), our proprietary method for guiding every patient from supported movement to full independence.

Function first. Environment second.
Water only earns its place when it serves the outcome.

The method underneath

FMAT™ — Functional Movement Aquatic Therapy Method™

+

A structured clinical system for how aquatic therapy is evaluated, progressed, and transitioned back to land-based function. Rather than exercises that happen to take place in water, FMAT uses buoyancy, resistance and depth intentionally — to restore movement, reduce stress on the body, and improve functional performance. Sessions progress through clearly defined stages of movement and loading, built around functional goals and measurable progression.

Covered in both Level 1 and Level 2 certification.

Pillar 01

Education & Certification

Level 1 builds the foundation: the therapeutic role and properties of water, safety, patient screening and suitability, and clinical application.

Level 2 advances aquatic assessment, clinical reasoning, treatment progression and skilled application in more complex rehabilitation presentations.
