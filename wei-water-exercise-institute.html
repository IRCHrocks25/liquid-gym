Why Water Works

Why Water Matters. We Already Decoded How.

Three things happen the moment you step into water, and none of them are guesswork.

Buoyancy

up to 90%of body weight removed

Buoyancy removes up to 90 percent of your body weight. Movement that hurts on land becomes possible again, not because the pain is gone, but because the load causing it finally is.

Viscosity

360°resistance in every direction

Viscosity gives you resistance in every direction at once, gentle enough to control, and real enough to build strength.

Hydrostatic pressure

eases swellingwhere land-based exercise aggravates it

Hydrostatic pressure, the steady pressure water places on the body, is part of why submerged movement can ease swelling that land-based exercise tends to aggravate instead.

This is not a softer version of exercise.
It is an alternative version.
