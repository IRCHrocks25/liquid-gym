Heart Wise Certification

Heart Wise Exercise Certified.

LiquidGym is a Heart Wise Exercise designated clinic. Our therapists are Heart Wise-certified, a programme run with the University of Ottawa Heart Institute.

What a clinic has to do to hold Heart Wise certification

  • Encourage regular aerobic exercise
  • Encourage and incorporate warm-up and cool-down, with self-monitoring through all exercise sessions
  • Allow participants to exercise at a safe level, with options to modify intensity
  • Include participants with chronic health conditions; approval from your physician may be needed
  • Offer health screening
  • Maintain a documented emergency plan known to all staff, including current CPR certification, phone access to local paramedic services, and a defibrillator on site
Why Water May Be an Option

What the Water Makes Possible.

Water immersion allows patients to perform movements they may be unable to perform on land. Exercises can be performed with greater ease and less pain because of the buoyancy the water creates.

The upward force of buoyancy counterbalances the downward effects of gravity and greatly decreases the impact forces your body is subjected to in traditional land therapy.

up to 80%

Standing chest-deep in water, your body weight can be reduced by up to 80 percent.

200 40 lb

Someone weighing 200 pounds on land may weigh as little as 40 pounds in the water, which is what makes it possible to safely perform exercise therapy that land would not allow.

Before and After Surgery

Preparing for Surgery, and Recovering From It.

Whether you are going in for cardiovascular surgery, a prehab programme may help with your recovery and post-surgical outcomes.

It is never too early to start pre-op therapy, also known as prehab, but starting at least 4 to 6 weeks prior to your scheduled surgery is recommended.

Post-op therapy through hydrotherapy can generally begin once the incision has healed, which can be between 15 and 30 days depending on the surgery. Ask your doctor when they would like you to start.
